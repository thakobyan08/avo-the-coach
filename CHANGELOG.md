# Changelog

All notable changes to Avo are listed here.
The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- Menu bar app with an avocado icon and pause states.
- Nudge card: a non-activating floating panel with five states (shows up, in progress, done, snoozed, pill) and a How to.
- 8 moves with pose art.
- Active-time tracking, plus Away and Quiet (camera, mic, full-screen app, work hours). No permissions needed.
- Scheduler with every spec timing configurable.
- Exercise rotation: no repeats of the last 2, and Walkabout at least every 3rd.
- Daily count, goal and streak, stored locally.
- Settings: General, Schedule, Moves and Timing, with pop-up menus of plain-language presets, plus launch at login.
- One-line install: `install.sh` downloads the universal app (Apple Silicon and Intel), with no Xcode needed.
